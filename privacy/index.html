Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Skypainter (“Skypainter”, “we”, “us”) is a drawing app for iPhone. This policy explains what the app handles and what it does not.

Summary

Skypainter does not require an account, does not sell personal data, and does not include advertising or analytics. Paintings and settings stay on your iPhone. We do not operate a server that receives your drawings.

Information on your device

  • Paintings. Strokes, sculpted terrain, and library previews are stored in the app’s documents on your device.
  • Settings. Brush, camera, and look preferences are stored on your device so they are still there the next time you open the app.
  • Photos. If you export a still, iOS asks permission to add that image to your photo library. The app only writes the picture you export. It does not read the rest of your library.

What we do not do

We do not collect contact information, location, identifiers for advertising, or usage analytics. We do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Children

Skypainter is not directed at collecting personal information from children. The app does not ask for an account.
