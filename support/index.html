Support

Skypainter needs iOS 26. Paintings are stored on the iPhone. Deleting the app deletes that library.

Drawing

Press and drag to steer. How far you move from where you started sets the speed. Lift your finger and the stroke coasts to a stop. Pinch in to sculpt the globe, and pinch back out to paint the sky.

Export

The share button saves a still of the current view. The first time, iOS asks permission to add photos. You can change that later in Settings → Skypainter → Photos.
