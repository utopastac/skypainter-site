Drawing for iOS

Paint a sky you can orbit.

Skypainter is a drawing app for painting light across a sky and a globe. No account. Paintings stay on your device.

  • Steer

    Direction follows your finger from the centre of the screen. How far you press sets the speed, and the stroke coasts when you lift.

  • Sculpt

    Pinch in to shape the globe, then pinch back out to paint the sky. Change the brush, colour, material, light, and sky.

  • Keep

    The library stays on the device. Share a still when you want a picture outside the app.

A drawing app for iPhone and iPad. Needs iOS 26.
